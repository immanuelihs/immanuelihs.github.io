WAVE 02 · 2022–2024

Domain-specific vision-LLMs, before they were standard practice

At a CTO office I bet early on domain-specific vision-language models — continual pretraining and QLoRA on hybrid ViT backbones so they could genuinely read CAD assemblies, P&ID and electrical drawings. That work went to production extracting from 300K+ engineering documents for Fortune 500 industrial manufacturers, plus a self-improving CAD QC agent worth a 40% designer-productivity gain. Four patents came out of this wave: hybrid vision encoders, learnable vision-LLMs for engineering drawings, mechanical-domain continual pretraining, and LLM-based medical report generation.

THE CONSTANT · 2022–now

The efficiency thread that runs through everything

Every wave had the same constraint: the model must fit the hardware in the room. I patented multi-criteria structured pruning — 70% compression with under 1% accuracy loss — and automated model verification, five published US applications in all, then extended the same instinct to training-free self-speculative decoding on H200s: lossless 1.34–1.67× latency reduction (2.88× peak) across Gemma, Qwen3, and Mistral. The same discipline puts a 100 ms hazard-detection loop on a Jetson Orin Nano in a product line launching 2027.

WAVE 03 · 2024–now

Agent verification & faithfulness — the current frontier

Now I work on whether you can trust what an agent says — both halves of it. ProvenanceGuard verifies an agent's claims against the sources it actually used (+47% grounding accuracy). Lacuna catches the failure nobody else checks: facts silently dropped, not fabricated (omission F1 0.603→0.747, beating an LLM-judge baseline at ~20× smaller). TraceForge hardens validated agent behavior into replayable DAGs — 73% of production traffic served from frozen workflows. My latest paper attributes omissions layer-by-layer through air-gapped pipelines and finds 68% originate in deterministic middleware, not the model. Deployed for an enterprise technology partner and a European national government; presenting at the Agentic AI Summit, UC Berkeley.
